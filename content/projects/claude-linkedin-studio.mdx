An open-source toolkit for writing and publishing LinkedIn posts straight from Claude. Share a rough idea in a chat, Claude shapes it into a post in your voice, checks it against your real experience, and publishes it to your LinkedIn profile when you say "publish it". Everything runs on your own accounts — no extra AI API keys, no central server, no telemetry.

## Features

- **Ideas to drafts from any device**: drop a rough thought, a few bullets, a voice-note transcript or a link into a Claude chat on desktop or phone
- **Iterative polishing**: one focused draft, then plain-language feedback ("shorter", "less formal", "lead with the second point") until it's right
- **Your own voice**: role, opinions, writing style, real stories and boundaries live in five reference files plus Project instructions — and Claude can interview you to fill them in
- **Strict authenticity rules**: no invented anecdotes, results, numbers or opinions; when a story is missing, Claude asks for one instead of making it up
- **Research mode**: Claude looks into what's new in your field and drafts a post with sources listed and facts attributed, while the opinion stays yours
- **Scheduled research and drafting**: recurring tasks like "every Monday, research my topics and draft one post for review" — a fresh draft is waiting for you, and you decide what goes live
- **One-sentence publishing**: "publish it" sends the exact approved text to your feed; deleting a post works the same way, and accidental double posts are blocked

## How it works

The project has two halves. **The Claude side** is a Claude Project holding the instructions and reference files — drafting, feedback, research and scheduling all happen there. **The connector** is a small Next.js app you deploy to your own Vercel account. It stores your LinkedIn connection encrypted in Neon Postgres and exposes a remote MCP server with three tools: `linkedin_status`, `publish_post` and `delete_post`.

Security is locked down by default, since the app can post publicly as you:

- **Single owner**: the console uses Google sign-in (OpenID Connect with PKCE, state and nonce) and only admits one verified email
- **Authenticated MCP endpoint**: claude.ai connects via OAuth 2.1 with Dynamic Client Registration, PKCE (S256) and rotating refresh tokens; Claude Code can use a static bearer token compared in constant time
- **Secrets stay server-side**: the LinkedIn token is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, and OAuth codes and tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes
- **Minimal LinkedIn scopes** (`openid profile w_member_social`), one-click kill switches, and every publish attempt logged

## Built within LinkedIn's rules

- **Official API, with your consent**: posts go through LinkedIn's Posts API via the self-serve *Share on LinkedIn* product, after you authorize your own app with OAuth — no browser automation, cookies or scraping
- **No automated posting**: LinkedIn's API Terms prohibit using the APIs to automate posting, so every post is published on your explicit request in a chat, and scheduled tasks only research and draft
- **Your own app, your own agreement**: each user creates their own LinkedIn developer app and accepts LinkedIn's terms directly; the repo never sees anyone's data
- **Only your own posts, minimal data**: the connector publishes and deletes posts on your profile — it never reads the feed, profiles, messages or connections — and stores only your member ID, an encrypted token and a log of what you published

## Use cases

- Turning half-formed ideas into consistent LinkedIn content without context-switching out of Claude
- Keeping a personal brand authentic — every post grounded in your own stories and opinions
- A weekly content routine where research and drafts are ready for review, and you approve every post
- A reference implementation of a self-hosted, OAuth-secured remote MCP server on Next.js and Vercel
